RANSOMWARE

Ransomware is a general term for a type of computer virus that blocks access to a computer system or computer files. The criminals controlling the computer virus demand payment – or ransom – in return for removing the virus and restoring access to the infected systems or files. Usually, the demand requires payment in cryptocurrency (bitcoin) because it is untraceable.

WHY IT CAN ADD UP

  • Ransomware amount (if paid) 
  • Forensic investigation
  • Lost income due to network interruption 
  • Virus removal
  • Data restoration
  • PR/customer communications costs
